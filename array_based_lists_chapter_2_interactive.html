Memory vs. logical order

The difference between physical cells and the List interface

A List exposes values at indices 0 through n−1. ArrayQueue and ArrayDeque may begin at physical index j ≠ 0. The diagrams show the backing array, while the line beneath reports its logical sequence.

Amortized analysis

Why resizing is affordable

Doubling on growth and shrinking when substantially underfull cost O(n) for one resize, but not for every operation. Over a sequence of updates, the total copying cost stays linear. A RootishArrayStack uses blocks of sizes 1, 2, …, b, with capacity b(b+1)/2 and O(√n) wasted cells.
