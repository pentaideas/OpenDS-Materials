Chapter 1 · Introduction

Interfaces & Complexity

Understand what collections promise and how algorithms behave as the input grows.

  • Stack, Queue, Deque, List, USet and SSet semantics
  • Interactive O(1), O(log n), O(n), O(n log n) and O(n²) comparisons
  • Random coin-flip experiments and correctness invariants
  • Highlighted explanations and step-by-step controls

Chapter 2 · Sections 2.1–2.6

Array-Based Lists

Watch elements shift, arrays resize and circular structures maintain their order.

  • ArrayStack and FastArrayStack bulk copies
  • ArrayQueue and ArrayDeque circular layouts
  • DualArrayDeque rebalancing and RootishArrayStack blocks
  • Live index, capacity and moved-cell metrics
Open Arrays Lab →
Chapter 3 · Sections 3.1–3.3

Linked Lists

Trace the references connecting nodes and see how blocks redistribute their contents.

  • SLList push, pop and FIFO operations
  • DLList sentinel, next and prev updates
  • SEList block insertion, borrowing and removal
  • Pointer walking and explanatory pseudocode
Open Linked Lists Lab →
Chapter 4 · Sections 4.1–4.4

Skiplists — Layer by Layer

Trace search paths through probabilistic levels, then work with both sorted sets and indexable lists.

  • Basic structure: sentinels, towers, and moving right or down
  • SkiplistSSet: successor search, insertion, removal, and coin-toss heights
  • SkiplistList: indexed get, set, insertion, and removal with span labels
  • Probabilistic heights and expected logarithmic search performance
Open Skiplist Lab →
Chapter 5 · Sections 5.1–5.3

Hash Tables — Buckets & Probing

Compare separate chaining and linear probing through animated collisions, search paths, deletion markers, and resizing.

  • ChainedHashTable: bucket lists, duplicate checks, and rehashing
  • LinearHashTable: consecutive probes, wraparound, and tombstones
  • Step-by-step insertion, search, deletion, and load-factor thresholds
  • Highlighted pseudocode with an explanation directly below each line
Open Hash Tables Lab →

  • Interactive practice questions and a guided vocabulary tour
