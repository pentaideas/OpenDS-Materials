6.2.4 · Complexity

Why “unbalanced” matters

Trees with the same number of nodes can have very different heights.

Balanced
3 edges

At most 4 nodes along a root-to-leaf search path.

One-sided chain
14 edges

Up to 15 nodes along a root-to-leaf search path.

For n nodes, searching, inserting, and deleting take O(h + 1), where h is tree height; §6.2 guarantees only O(n) worst case.
