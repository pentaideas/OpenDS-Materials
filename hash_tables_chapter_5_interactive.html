§5.3 · Hash codes & theory

A teaching hash, not a production hash

To make collisions predictable, this animation uses h(x) = x mod m (normalized for negative keys). Morin studies multiplicative and tabulation hashing to obtain more useful probabilistic guarantees. Real hash codes can map strings and objects to integers.

With suitable hashing and resizing, operations have expected O(1) amortized time, but a poor collision pattern can require O(n) work. The maximum chain length and probing runs matter more than the load factor alone.

Supplementary educational demo based on Pat Morin's Open Data Structures, Chapter 5 (§5.1 ChainedHashTable, §5.2 LinearHashTable, §5.3 Hash Codes). Pseudocode is adapted for teaching; the modular hash is intentionally simpler than Morin's randomized hashes.
